# Policy - likeness, voice & licenses

This file is the honest contract for MotionField's swap, character, lip-sync and
voice surfaces. The same rules back the code gates; where the UI and the
server disagree, the server wins.

## Allowed uploads

Upload only media you own or have permission to use - your own footage,
photos and voice recordings, or material licensed for this kind of editing.
Anything that shows or sounds like a real person needs that person's
permission (see likeness consent below). Server-side checks can't see intent,
so the uploader's assertion is the record we keep.

## Likeness consent

Any input that carries a real person's identity requires an explicit,
unticked-by-default assertion: **"I have the right to use this person's
likeness"**.

Identity points (all enforced server-side, so REST/v1/MCP/CLI get the same
400 as the UI):

- `POST /api/refs` when `kind` is `person` or `category` is `character` -
  the body must include `likeness_consent: true`, else `400 likeness
  consent required`. Character-studio and /characters send it from the
  checkbox; their upload controls stay disabled until it's ticked.
- `motionfield/wan-vace/character-swap`, `motionfield/wan-vace/motion-transfer`,
  `motionfield/wan-animate/character-swap` - the subject `image_urls`.
- `motionfield/re-edit/v1.0` - `image_urls` on every flow except `restyle`
  (character-swap places the subject; motion-transfer re-renders them
  performing the reference's motion). Consent is stored with the chain's
  first job and reused for per-shot regenerates.
- `motionfield/person-swap/v1.0` - the source `video_url` (the person being swapped
  or re-staged) AND the identity `image_urls` (the person-swap's face) - one
  consent covers the whole job, checked before any probe or VACE window runs.
- `motionfield/musetalk/lip-sync-video` - the source `video_url` (it is the
  person being re-voiced); `motionfield/musetalk/talking-portrait` and
  `talking-portrait-ltx`, `portrait-says` and `portrait-says-ltx`, and
  `motionfield/wan-s2v/talking-portrait` - the portrait `image_url`.

Implementation: `src/lib/likeness.ts` holds the endpoint→param map and the
`likenessConsentCheck` validate each workflow declares. The consent param
never reaches a graph - it exists only for the gate. Deep-link prefills
(`?params=`) can't pre-tick it - the studio strips the key before state.

## No impersonation or deceptive use

Don't use swap, character, lip-sync or voice tools to impersonate someone, to
put words in a real person's mouth, or to make deceptive media - no fake
endorsements, no fabricated statements, no passing generated output off as a
real recording. Consented parody, clearly-labeled edits and your own
likeness are what these surfaces are for.

## Output ownership & disclosure

You own what you generate, subject to each model's license - the catalog's
per-workflow license row (docs/LICENSES.md) is the authority; a restricted
class (e.g. LTX-Video's Open RAIL-M) is opt-in and its use terms ride with
the output. Content edited or generated by AI should be disclosed as such
when you share it - voice-clone output is watermarked when `resemble-perth`
is installed (`scripts/setup-voice.sh`), and the disclosure obligation stays
with you either way.

## Voice

- Chatterbox voice-clone output is watermarked when `resemble-perth` is
  installed (`scripts/setup-voice.sh`): the pack's inaudible Perth watermark
  rides every clone (`use_watermark: true` in
  `workflows/chatterbox-voice-clone.json`; `resemble-perth` - MIT - is
  pip-installed into the ComfyUI venv by the setup script). Only clone a
  voice you have the right to use.
- Kokoro TTS is fixed-voice (hexgrad, Apache-2.0) - no cloning, no consent
  gate.

- Wan 2.1 VACE 1.3B (Alibaba) - Apache-2.0. Swap, motion transfer, restyle,
  reframe, character motion stage.
- DWPose ONNX (IDEA-Research) - Apache-2.0. Landmark detection for character
  conditioning, `OF_MouthMask`, and the patched MuseTalk bbox path.
- MuseTalk (TMElyralab) - MIT. The local pack is patched
  (`scripts/patches/comfyui-musetalk-mps.patch` via `scripts/setup-musetalk.sh`):

  the mouth + lower-jaw mask from the same DWPose landmarks used for the
  bbox. S3FD (research-only) is gone too - the fallback bbox derives from
  DWPose face keypoints.
- Kokoro - Apache-2.0 (weights) / MIT (pack). Chatterbox - MIT (model and
  weights, Resemble AI); the local pack (wildminder's `ComfyUI-Chatterbox`)
 - Apache-2.0; Perth watermark - MIT.
- LTX-Video 0.9.5 - Open RAIL-M, **use-restricted**, opt-in only: the `*-ltx`
  workflows are labeled "opt-in" and gated by the license check
  (`assertLicenseAllowed`) for local runs.

## Characters are reference-based - no training

"Characters" and character refs store the user's own uploaded images as
`@name` references (elements table, `category=character` / `kind=person`).
Generation conditions on those images directly (VACE reference images /
multi-image i2i). There is no LoRA training, no per-character model weights,
and no `/api/characters` POST - that route is read-only.
